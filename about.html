Our Story

A decade of dedication to your recovery

Gaber Centers was founded in 2015 with a single vision: to provide world-class physiotherapy care to every patient who walks through our doors, regardless of their background or the complexity of their condition.

What began as a small clinic in Boulaq Al Dakrour has grown into one of Egypt's most trusted rehabilitation centers, with over 11,000 patients treated across a wide spectrum of orthopedic, neurological, and sports conditions.

Our approach has always been the same: listen deeply, assess thoroughly, treat precisely, and follow through completely. Every patient is a unique story, and every treatment plan is written with that story in mind.

Our Journey

A decade of milestones

2015
Foundation & First Patients
Opened our first clinic in Boulaq Al Dakrour, Giza, with a mission to provide evidence-based physiotherapy to the community.
2017
Expanded Services
Added neurological rehabilitation and sports physiotherapy programs, expanding our capacity to treat complex conditions.
2019
1,000 Patients Milestone
Celebrated treating our 1,000th patient — a moment that confirmed our commitment and the trust our community placed in us.
2021
Home Visit Program Launch
Launched our professional home visit service, bringing expert physiotherapy to patients unable to travel to our clinic.

100K Social Milestone
Reached 100,000 followers across social platforms, establishing Gaber Centers as a leading voice in Egyptian healthcare education.
2025
11,000+ Patients & Growing
Today we continue to grow — serving thousands of patients, expanding our team, and pursuing our mission of accessible, premium rehabilitation care.
Our Core Values

What drives everything we do

These principles guide every decision, every treatment, and every interaction at Gaber Centers.

🎯
Evidence-Based Practice

Every technique we use is grounded in the latest clinical research. We never stop learning, because our patients deserve nothing less than the best.

❤️
Patient-Centered Care

You are not a diagnosis. You are a person with goals, fears, and a life to return to. We build every treatment plan around who you are, not just what's wrong.

🔬
Precision & Expertise

Physiotherapy is both science and art. Our team combines technical mastery with clinical intuition to deliver precise, effective treatments.

🤝
Integrity & Honesty

We tell you what you need to hear, not what you want to hear. If surgery is necessary, we'll say so. If it isn't, we'll prove it through results.

🚀
Continuous Improvement

We invest in ongoing training, advanced equipment, and new techniques so that every patient benefits from the most current rehabilitation science.

🌍
Community Impact

We believe in giving back. Through our social platforms, we educate 116,000+ followers about injury prevention, recovery, and healthy living.
