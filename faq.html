General
What is physiotherapy and who needs it? +
Physiotherapy is a healthcare profession that uses physical methods — exercise, manual therapy, and electrotherapy — to restore movement and function. It benefits anyone with pain, injury, post-surgical recovery, neurological conditions, or sports injuries. If you have difficulty moving or experience chronic pain, physiotherapy can help.

Services
What conditions do you treat? +
We treat a wide range of conditions including: back and neck pain, disc herniation, osteoarthritis, post-surgical recovery, stroke rehabilitation, sports injuries, frozen shoulder, knee pain, sciatica, Parkinson's disease, and many more. If you're unsure, contact us and we'll advise you.
What is manual therapy? +
Manual therapy involves hands-on techniques performed by our physiotherapists to mobilize joints, release muscle tension, and improve circulation. It is highly effective for neck pain, back pain, joint stiffness, and headaches caused by cervical issues.
What is electrotherapy? +
Electrotherapy uses electrical stimulation devices such as TENS, ultrasound, and laser therapy to reduce pain, promote healing, and stimulate muscle function. It is a safe, non-invasive treatment used alongside other physiotherapy methods.
Do you treat children? +
Yes, we provide pediatric physiotherapy for children with developmental delays, neurological conditions, and orthopedic issues. Our team is experienced in creating child-friendly treatment plans that are both effective and engaging.
Can physiotherapy help avoid surgery? +
In many cases, yes. Evidence shows that conservative physiotherapy can eliminate the need for surgery in conditions like disc herniation, knee osteoarthritis, rotator cuff tears, and hip arthritis. We have documented cases where patients avoided surgery through our programs. However, this depends on the condition severity — we always provide honest advice.
Do you offer neurological rehabilitation? +
Yes. We specialize in neurological rehabilitation for stroke survivors, patients with Parkinson's disease, spinal cord injuries, and other neurological conditions. Our programs focus on restoring movement, improving balance, and maximizing independence in daily life.

Booking
How do I book an appointment? +
You can book through multiple channels: online via our booking page, by calling 010 2510 1874 or 011 2018 7048, or through WhatsApp. We recommend booking at least 24 hours in advance to secure your preferred slot.
Can I cancel or reschedule? +
Yes. Please notify us at least 4 hours before your appointment to cancel or reschedule without any charge. Late cancellations may affect your ability to book priority slots. Contact us via phone or WhatsApp.
What should I bring to my first appointment? +
For your first visit, please bring: any relevant medical reports, X-rays or MRI scans related to your condition, a list of current medications, and comfortable clothing that allows easy access to the area being treated. Arrive 10 minutes early to complete your initial assessment form.
How long is each session? +
A standard physiotherapy session lasts 45 to 60 minutes. The first assessment session may take up to 75 minutes as it includes a comprehensive evaluation of your condition. Session length may vary depending on your treatment plan.
💊
Treatment
How many sessions will I need? +
The number of sessions varies depending on your condition, severity, and goals. Acute injuries may resolve in 6–10 sessions, while chronic conditions or post-surgical recovery may require 20–30+ sessions. We provide a personalized estimate after your initial assessment.
Is physiotherapy painful? +
Some techniques may cause mild discomfort, particularly manual therapy and exercise therapy when targeting injured areas. However, physiotherapy should never be severely painful. Our therapists continuously monitor your comfort and adjust treatment accordingly. Any discomfort is temporary and part of the healing process.
Will I receive exercises to do at home? +
Yes. A key part of your recovery is a home exercise program tailored to your condition. Our therapists will teach you specific exercises and provide clear instructions. Consistency with home exercises significantly accelerates your recovery and maintains long-term results.
What is your success rate? +
We maintain a 98% patient satisfaction rate across all cases. Our documented outcomes show significant improvement in pain levels, mobility, and quality of life for the vast majority of patients. Visit our Cases page to see real patient stories and outcomes.
